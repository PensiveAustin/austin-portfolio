Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to build a website?

It depends on the number of pages and features. A simple site is much quicker than one with logins, a database or AI features. Tell me what you need and I'll give you an estimate before we start.

Will my website work on mobile?

Yes. Every site I build is responsive, so it looks and works properly on phones, tablets and desktops.

Can you add a database, login or admin dashboard?

Yes. I build custom databases, user accounts and admin dashboards for sites that need to store and manage real data.

Can you handle the domain and hosting?

Yes. I can set up your domain and hosting and deploy your site through Cloudflare, so you don't have to figure it out yourself.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. Send me the link and tell me what isn't working, and we'll decide together what to keep and what to rebuild.

What do you need to get started?

A short description of your business or idea, what you want the site to do, and any logo, photos or example sites you like. If you don't have these yet, we can start with a conversation.
